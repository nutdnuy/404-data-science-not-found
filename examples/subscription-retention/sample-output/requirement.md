# Requirement

- **Decision:** rank voluntary subscription churn within 30 days to help a human review a fictional retention queue.
- **Owners:** fictional product and data-owner roles.
- **Success:** a reproducible run, honest fixed-baseline comparison, and visible leakage controls.
- **Scope:** locally generated synthetic subscriptions; no real customers, intervention, profit claim, or automated action.
- **Unresolved for real use:** capacity, intervention cost, consent, and deployment approval.
